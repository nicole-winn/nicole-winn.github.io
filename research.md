## Current Research

### STI Testing Preferences and Barriers (Brown University, 2025–Present)

- Completed analysis of a discrete choice experiment (DCE) examining preferences for STI testing attributes including location, test type, result method, and time to receive results
- Conducting semi-structured interviews with community members in Providence, RI
- Asking about personal-level and community-level barriers and facilitators to STI testing
- Mixed-methods approach combining quantitative preference data with qualitative thematic analysis
